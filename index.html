12-WEEK TARGET & CHECKPOINTS

Age 28; 160 lb; 6 ft 0.5 in; variable activity (desk days and demanding camera shoots); weight stable; beginner lifter; knee currently symptom-free; estimated 13–15% fat. Start at ~2,600 kcal / 160 g protein / 330 g carbs / 70 g fat (2,590 kcal from macros). This is a trial intake, not a measured maintenance requirement. Desk days: begin at 2,600 kcal. On unusually long physically demanding shoots, consider an additional 200–400 kcal of food based on hunger, duration and exertion; do not automatically add calories for every shoot. Review two-week average weight, waist, and performance before changing the baseline. Goal: visually leaner, stronger, better chest/shoulders/legs; 160–165 lb provisional weight range. Do not chase an arbitrary number.


Check weekly average weight, gym performance and recovery. Check waist and comparable photos every two weeks. Adjust intake 100–150 kcal only after two weeks of trend data.


Weeks 1–2 establish technique with only 2 working sets per exercise, stopping with 2–3 reps in reserve; 3–6 progress to prescribed sets if recovering; 7 deload; 8–12 progress again. No need to train to failure. 12 weeks ends January 28, 2027.

TRAVEL: BODYWEIGHT + 20–50 LB BANDS

Keep Monday/Tuesday/Thursday/Friday split. Use a secure door anchor, checking its rating and door direction. Rest 60–120 sec between sets. Choose band tension leaving 1–3 reps in reserve.

MEASURED DAILY PORTIONS — START HERE

Use these as a repeatable starting template, not exact nutritional facts. Product labels, cooking loss, and fat percentage change the totals. Aim for ~2,600 kcal and 145–175 g protein by adjusting the final top-up.


WEEK A — MON/WED/FRI/SUN

8:30 Breakfast: 3 eggs, 70 g dry oats, 100 g berries
10:15 Shake: 1 label serving protein powder + 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
1:00 Lunch: 5 oz cooked 93% turkey, 1.5 cups cooked rice, 1/2 cup black beans, 1–2 cups vegetables, 1 tsp olive oil
3:45 Snack: 1 slice whole wheat bread + 2 tbsp PB, 1 banana
6:30 Dinner: 5 oz cooked chicken, 300 g cooked sweet potato, 2 cups broccoli, 2 tsp olive oil
9:00 Top-up: 170 g Greek yogurt and fruit/granola as needed

WEEK A — TUE/THU/SAT

8:30 Breakfast: 250 g plain Greek yogurt, 70 g granola, 100 g berries
10:15 Shake: protein powder + almond milk
1:00 Lunch: turkey, rice, beans and vegetables as above
3:45 Snack: PB foldover + banana
6:30 Dinner: 6 oz tilapia or salmon, 1.5 cups cooked rice or 300 g potato, salad with 1 tbsp olive oil
9:00 Top-up: oats or yogurt as needed. Use salmon at least once per week.

WEEK B — MON/WED/FRI/SUN

8:30 Breakfast: 250 g Greek yogurt, 60 g oats, 1 banana, 15 g walnuts
10:15 Shake: protein powder + almond milk
1:00 Lunch: 5 oz cooked chicken, 1 cup cooked lentils, 250 g potato, spinach
3:45 Snack: PB foldover + fruit
6:30 Dinner: 7 oz firm tofu, 1 cup edamame, 1.5 cups cooked rice, 2 cups stir-fry vegetables, 1 tsp sesame oil
9:00 Top-up: Greek yogurt or oats as needed

WEEK B — TUE/THU/SAT

8:30 Breakfast: 3 eggs, 2 slices whole wheat toast, 1 cup pineapple
10:15 Shake: protein powder + almond milk
1:00 Lunch: chicken-lentil stew with potato as above
3:45 Snack: PB foldover + banana
6:30 Dinner: 6 oz tilapia or salmon, 1.5 cups cooked quinoa, 2 cups vegetables, 1 tbsp olive oil
9:00 Top-up: Greek yogurt/granola as needed. Use salmon at least once per week.


Flexible meal: replace one lunch or dinner weekly with pizza, ramen, Chick-fil-A or a restaurant meal. Eat normally the rest of the day; do not punish yourself with extra cardio. For the first 14 days, log portions and adjust the optional top-up to fit the calorie target.

ALTERNATING WEEKLY MEALS

Week A (weeks 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11): Breakfast eggs + oats + berries; lunch turkey/black-bean bowls with rice and spinach; dinner slow-cooker soy-garlic chicken with rice and broccoli. Swap 1–2 dinners for salmon or tilapia with sweet potato. Snack: PB foldover, fruit, protein shake, and yogurt if needed.


Week B (weeks 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12): Breakfast Greek yogurt, granola, banana, and oats as needed; lunch chicken-lentil stew with potato; dinner tofu-edamame stir fry with quinoa or rice. Swap 1–2 dinners for tilapia and sweet potato, and include fatty fish once. Same PB foldover and shake daily.


Portion starting guide: 5–6 oz cooked meat/fish or ~7 oz tofu plus 1 cup edamame; 1–1.5 cups cooked rice/quinoa or ~250–350 g potato; 1–2 cups vegetables. Measure all cooking oils and sauces. Fish and tofu meals may require extra protein from yogurt or another food. Track for 14 days to calibrate actual calories.


Flexible meal: one pizza, ramen, restaurant or Chick-fil-A meal each week. Eat normally the rest of the day; no compensatory restriction.


Carb alternative: quinoa, whole-grain pasta, beans, lentils, bread and potatoes. PB: regular or reduced-fat Jif both work; weigh a 2 tbsp serving because a large scoop can be substantially more. Coffee: generally up to 400 mg caffeine daily, less if sensitive, and avoid late in the day.
